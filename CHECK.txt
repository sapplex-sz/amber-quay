Known limitations
- Not tested on touch devices or with gamepads; desktop keyboard and mouse only.
- Traffic has no lane changes or overtaking; cars queue behind blockers and "ghost through" after a long stall.
- Police pathing follows the grid road graph and switches to direct pursuit with line of sight, so cruisers can occasionally jam against props.
- Tall buildings go see-through when they would hide the player or car; other entities behind tall buildings can still be hidden.
- Audio starts after the first key press or click (browser autoplay rules). All sound is synthesised; there is no sound file.
- Fonts fall back to system condensed sans-serif faces if Oswald / Barlow are not installed; layout still holds.
